Airplane
Place it, right click to get in. W/S speed up and slow down, arrow keys steer (↑ climb, ↓ dive, ←→ turn), V autopilots to the nearest villager's house, Shift to get out
Nether
Build an obsidian frame 4 wide and 5 tall, light it with flint and steel, and walk in. Piglins there trade for gold ingots.
The End
Craft an eye of ender (ender pearl + glowstone) and right click the ground with it to open an End portal. Jump in, break the crystals on the towers, then beat the Ender Dragon.
Bow
Hold right click to pull back, let go to shoot. Survival needs arrows (1 stick + 1 cobblestone makes 4).
Ender pearl
Right click to teleport to where you look
OneBlock
In the pause menu, press Play OneBlock: you stand on a single block in the sky. Break it and a new one appears, going from plains to underground, snow, beach, village, the Nether and the End.
Sleep
Right click a bed at night to skip to morning. It also sets where you respawn.
Kill
K instantly kills the animal or monster you look at (or the nearest one)
Chat
T or Enter to talk (to everyone in a multiplayer room). Commands: /help, /find, /tp NAME (go to a friend), /day, /night, /seed, /spawn, /fly
