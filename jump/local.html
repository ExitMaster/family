🚀 How to play

👆Press your key (or tap your own strip of the screen) to blast off.
⏱️Hold it down to fly higher. Let go early and you only do a little hop — that's how you sneak under things.
🔥Tap again in mid-air for a jetpack boost — it shoves you further. Right now your fuel is endless (🔥 ∞), so you can keep tapping and keep climbing.
📦Clear a box safely: +1 point.
⭐Just touch a floating star and it's yours: +3 stars. Brushing past counts — you don't have to land. Spend them in the avatar shop.
🛬Coming down on a box counts as a landing, even a messy diagonal one. Only a proper smack into the side hurts.
♥You have 3 hearts (top right of your strip). A side-smack costs one heart, bounces you on top of the box, and you blink for a second where nothing can hurt you. Lose all 3 and you're out.
⛶Press F or the ⛶ button for full screen.
🏰Every so often a giant tower rears up with a red flag on top. Its star is worth 12 stars — blast your way up there and grab it.
🐦A bird hovering in the air. Clip it and it swats you down for a heart — birds sit low and right up by the ceiling, so you can't just fly along the top any more.
🕳️A hole in the ground. Jump it! Fall in and you scramble back out, but it costs a heart. There's a star floating over every hole.
🛗A sliding platform (the blue one with arrows). It drifts up and down and always floats over your head, so you can run under it — or land on it for a star.
