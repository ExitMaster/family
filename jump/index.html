🚀 How to play

👆Press SPACE (or tap the screen) to blast off.
⏱️Hold it down to fly higher. Let go early and you only do a little hop.
🔥Tap again in mid-air for a jetpack boost — it shoves you further. You get 3 per flight (the orange dots). They refill the moment you touch down.
📦Clear a box safely: +1 point.
⭐Land neatly on top of a box: +3 stars — spend them in the shop below.
💥Smack into the side of a box and you're out. Everyone else keeps flying.
⛶Press F or the ⛶ button for full screen.
